# Software Subscription Agreement

Between Acme Data Co. ("Provider") and the subscribing organization ("Customer").

## 1. Services
Provider will make the Acme reporting platform available to Customer during the Term in accordance with the documentation.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Overdue amounts may accrue interest at the lesser of 1% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 3. Term and Renewal
The initial term is one (1) year. This Agreement will automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods. Provider will notify Customer in writing no less than sixty (60) days prior to each renewal date, and Customer may elect not to renew by giving written notice at any time before the renewal date.

## 4. Termination
Customer may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Upon such termination, Provider will refund any prepaid fees covering the remainder of the term after the effective date of termination.

Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and use it only to perform under this Agreement.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement will exceed the total amounts paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement during the twelve (12) month period preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
