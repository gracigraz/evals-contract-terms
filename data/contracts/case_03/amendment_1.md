# Amendment No. 1 to the Brightline Cloud Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") modifies the Brightline Cloud Services Agreement between Brightline Software, Inc. and Customer (the "Agreement"). If this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment controls.

1. Section 6 (Limitation of Liability) is deleted and replaced with: "Brightline's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the one (1) month before the event giving rise to the claim. Customer's liability under this Agreement is not limited."

2. Section 2 (Fees and Payment) is deleted and replaced with: "Invoices are due upon receipt, and late amounts accrue interest at five percent (5%) per month."

3. All other terms of the Agreement remain in effect.
