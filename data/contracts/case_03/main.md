# Brightline Cloud Services Agreement

Between Brightline Software, Inc. ("Brightline") and the subscribing customer ("Customer").

## 1. Subscription
Brightline grants Customer a subscription to the Brightline workflow platform for the subscription term.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Invoices are payable within forty-five (45) days of Customer's receipt. Brightline may charge interest on late amounts at up to one percent (1%) per month.

## 3. Term; Automatic Renewal
Each subscription term lasts twelve (12) months and renews automatically for an additional twelve (12) months. At least sixty (60) days before any renewal, Brightline will send Customer written notice of the upcoming renewal. Customer may cancel the renewal by written notice at any time before the renewal date.

## 4. Termination for Convenience
Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time, without cause, by giving Brightline thirty (30) days' written notice. Brightline will refund the unused, prepaid portion of the fees on a pro-rata basis.

## 5. Data Security
Brightline will maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer data.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
