# Corvid Cloud Master Services Agreement

Between Corvid Cloud LLC ("Corvid") and the customer identified in the Order Form ("Customer").

## 1. Services
Corvid will provide the hosted Corvid storage service described in the Order Form.

## 2. Payment
Customer will pay invoices within forty-five (45) days after receiving them. Late balances may bear interest of up to 1% per month.

## 3. Renewal
This Agreement renews automatically for consecutive one-year terms. Corvid will give Customer written notice at least sixty (60) days before each renewal date, and Customer may cancel the upcoming renewal by written notice at any time before that date.

## 4. Termination for Convenience
Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice. All fees paid are non-refundable, including fees prepaid for any period after termination.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
IN NO EVENT WILL CORVID'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED ONE HUNDRED U.S. DOLLARS ($100).
